What you'll learn

Week by Week

WEEK 01

AI Foundations — Meet Your Tools

  • What ChatGPT and Claude actually are, explained in plain language
  • Where each tool is stronger, and when to reach for which one
  • Setting up and navigating both platforms with confidence
WEEK 02

Prompt Engineering Essentials

  • The anatomy of a prompt that actually gets a useful answer
  • Giving examples the AI can learn your style from
  • Turning a flat, generic answer into something sharp
WEEK 03

AI for Design & Creative Work

  • Writing prompts for briefs, taglines, and creative concepts
  • Prompting image tools like Midjourney and DALL-E with precision
  • Building a social media carousel from idea to finished copy
WEEK 04

Applied Projects & Capstone

  • Chaining prompts into a real, repeatable workflow
  • Building your own project: a brand kit, portfolio piece, or content pack
  • Peer feedback, instructor review, and certification
